I have seen a ton of anime, so it is hard to really say what my favorites are. I have been watching for so long that i can't remember some shows that I really liked. If I had to list a top 12 in no order, it would probably be:
Gintama, Steins Gate, Lovely Complex, GTO, AKira, Clannad+Afterstory, Trigun, FLCL season 1, Ouran Host Club, March Comes Like A Lion, Fullmetal Alchemist Brotherhood and 2003, and of course all of the Ghibli movies. I have been a Ghibli fan since I was about 4 years old and they really mean a lot to me.
Some lesser known titles that I always have to suggest to people:
Noein, Last exile, Darker Than Black, Wolf's Rain, Gankutsuo the Count of Monte Cristo, Tatami Galaxy, Gosick, and Hana Yori Dango.
