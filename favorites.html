▼Favorite movies
I have lost interest in most western movies in recent years, as I feel like there is a lot of the same kind of stuff coming out that I have already seen 1000 times growing up. However, there are still plenty of really good ones. I won't be adding any anime movies to this list.
Some of my past favorites include: Fight Club, Swiss Army Man, The Shining, The Usual Suspects, Clockwork Orange, 2001 a Space Odyssey (I am a Kubrick fan for sure haha), Inception, Interstellar, The lord of the Rings trilogy, 28 Days Later, Up, 12 Monkeys, and recently I really enjoyed the Christopher Nolan Odyssey movie.
Some of my favorites growing up were:
The Black Cauldron, The Indian in the Cupboard, Jumanji (the original of course), The Pagemaster, The Secret of NIMH, all the classic Disney movies, the first Harry Potter movie, the classic Pixar films, Milo and Otis, and more that i can't think of off the top in my head. If you have not seen The Black Cauldron definitely check it out! It is certainly Disney's darkest film, and certainly gave me nightmares as a very little kid, but it is super cool and I loved it anyway. Very 80's sword and sorcery vibes. My biggest issue with modern Disney is that they don't do hand drawn animation anymore, and I just dont have any interest in the cheap looking computer animation that they do. It does not age nearly as well either.

To get this out of the way now, as someone who is LGBT myself, I still love Harry potter. Obviously I do not support or agree with anything J.K.R says but I am certainly not going to let her take away one of the most important parts of my childhood because she is being an ignoramus maximus. If anyone disagrees with my decision on this, or wants to police what content I consume, they can keep it to themselves because I don't have time for it.
