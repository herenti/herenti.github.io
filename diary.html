▼Struggling lately, on the importance of faith in my life - 9/2/2026
Lately I have been mainly dealing with depression and feeling hurt by not ever having had a relationship in my 31 years of life. One of most important things I do have though is my faith in love and the Divine. To me, there can be no better way to live than to live with love, and to not be in doubt about love. The more I do this, the happier I am regardless of what is happening in my life. Lately I have been seeing the importance of moving beyond doubt and not letting myself fall into some kind of nihilism. To me, nihilism and atheism are still faiths. I would be convinced of the way things are without really knowing the truth, and such a miserable faith to have! To me however there can be no greater faith than love, and such a faith has been said to be a way to true wisdom.

Living in a state of constant doubt has been a cause of great suffering and anxiety for me lately. I hope soon that the dawn comes on this long night, and I won't be crushed under this heavy weight any more. I have doubt about what happens after death, and other numerous existential worries. It's all nonsense though and sometimes I break through the cloud of fear and I get a big smile on my face and feel love in my heart. Maybe I should have a formal spiritual practice so I can continue to improve, or maybe all I can do is surrender and see what comes my way.

Temperatures have dropped this week so I should go hiking! As long as it's not pouring down rain... we need rain though. Also they are playing the Ghibli movies at a local theater. I have seen them 100000 times but maybe I should go see one again. Also I need to remind myself to read more good books instead of wasting my time watching dumb videos on youtube.
